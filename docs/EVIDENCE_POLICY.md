# MedicoBuddy AI — Evidence Policy & Grounding Standards

## Evidence Tiering & Source Requirements

1. **Tier 1 (High Quality Guidelines & Systematic Reviews):**
   - World Health Organization (WHO) Guidelines
   - National Library of Medicine (NLM) MedlinePlus Consumer Health Topics
   - Cochrane Systematic Reviews
   - PubMed Central Open Access Peer-Reviewed Literature

2. **Tier 2 (Traditional Practice & Expert Guidance):**
   - Ministry of AYUSH Non-Pharmacological Lifestyle Guidelines
   - Peer-reviewed observational sleep/hydration studies

## Ingestion & Quarantine Governance

- **Licence Validation:** Material without explicit Open Access, Public Domain, or CC-BY licences is rejected.
- **Quarantine Trigger Rules:**
  - Prompt injection code blocks or hidden instructions
  - Retracted studies or published corrections
  - Prohibited clinical claims (cure claims, essential oil ingestion, oral herbal formulations, dosage advice)
- **Claim-to-Citation Entailment:** Any sentence generated by the LLM that cannot be traced to a retrieved passage is automatically stripped during output validation.
