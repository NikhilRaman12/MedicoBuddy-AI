# MedicoBuddy — Clinician Review Checklist

## Pre-Deployment Clinical Review

This checklist must be completed by a qualified healthcare professional before any public-facing deployment.

### 1. Safety Engine Review
- [ ] All 23 red-flag rules reviewed for medical accuracy
- [ ] Red-flag patterns cover all major emergency presentations
- [ ] No false negatives in benchmark red-flag scenarios (100% required)
- [ ] False positive rate is acceptable for the use case
- [ ] Escalation messaging is clear, calm, and appropriate
- [ ] Emergency contact numbers are correct for all configured regions

### 2. Scope Boundaries Review
- [ ] Target population (adults 18–65) is appropriate
- [ ] Exclusion criteria (children, elderly, pregnant, immunocompromised) are complete
- [ ] Symptom scope correctly limited to mild, short-duration conditions
- [ ] Out-of-scope redirections are medically appropriate

### 3. Self-Care Guidance Review
- [ ] All recommended comfort measures are genuinely low-risk
- [ ] Hydration advice accounts for fluid restrictions (heart/kidney)
- [ ] Dietary suggestions account for diabetes, allergies, intolerances
- [ ] No pharmacological recommendations present
- [ ] "When to seek care" conditions are medically appropriate
- [ ] Monitoring guidance is clinically sound

### 4. Ayurveda Content Review
- [ ] No oral formulations, supplements, or medicinal-dose herbs recommended
- [ ] Evidence labels accurately reflect study evidence
- [ ] "Traditional use" never presented as "proven effective"
- [ ] Risk information is disclosed where evidence exists
- [ ] Practices recommended are genuinely non-pharmacological and low-risk

### 5. Language and Communication Review
- [ ] Hedged language used appropriately ("may help," "some evidence suggests")
- [ ] No diagnostic claims ("you have," "this is")
- [ ] No cure guarantees
- [ ] Disclaimer is prominent and accurate
- [ ] Language is accessible and culturally appropriate
- [ ] Emergency messaging conveys appropriate urgency without causing panic

### 6. Evidence Quality Review
- [ ] Evidence hierarchy correctly implemented (guidelines > SRs > RCTs > ...)
- [ ] Retracted papers are excluded
- [ ] Blog/popular sources cannot override clinical evidence
- [ ] Evidence confidence levels are honestly reported
- [ ] Citations link to real, verifiable sources

### 7. Contraindication Coverage Review
- [ ] Diabetes-related contraindications are complete
- [ ] Kidney disease restrictions are appropriate
- [ ] Heart disease / hypertension considerations are adequate
- [ ] Allergy checking is functional
- [ ] Medication interaction awareness is appropriate

### 8. Privacy and Ethics Review
- [ ] No personally identifiable information collected unnecessarily
- [ ] Consent flow is clear and functional
- [ ] Data retention is appropriate
- [ ] Chat history opt-in (not default)
- [ ] PII redaction in logs is effective
