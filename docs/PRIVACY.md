# MedicoBuddy AI — Privacy Notice & Data Retention

## Truthful Privacy Statement

- **Data Processing:** User queries are processed in-memory for the duration of the conversation thread.
- **No Long-term PII Storage:** MedicoBuddy AI does not store user names, phone numbers, or explicit personal identifiers in log files.
- **Thread Control:** Users may delete conversation state at any time via the "Clear Conversation" action in the UI.
- **Not HIPAA-Certified:** MedicoBuddy AI is an educational self-care assistant and is not certified as a HIPAA covered entity or medical device.
