# MedicoBuddy — Sample Conversations

## 1. Safe Scenario: Mild Headache (Self-Care Guidance)

**User:** I've had a mild headache since this morning. I'm 32 years old, no allergies, no medications.

**MedicoBuddy:**
> **✅ Self-care information**
>
> **Summary:** You have reported a mild headache since this morning (intensity: mild).
>
> **Steps That May Provide Comfort:**
> - Rest in a quiet, dimly lit room — reducing sensory stimulation may help ease discomfort
> - Stay hydrated with small, frequent sips of plain water
> - A cool cloth on the forehead may provide temporary relief
> - Gentle deep breathing (inhale 4 seconds, hold 4, exhale 6) may help with relaxation
> - Temporarily reduce strenuous physical activity
>
> **🌿 Ayurveda-Informed Perspective:**
> - **Warm water sipping** — *Limited/Preliminary Evidence* — Sipping warm water is a traditional Ayurvedic recommendation; limited studies suggest warm water may aid comfort
> - **Consistent sleep-wake routine (dinacharya)** — *Evidence Supported* — Maintaining regular sleep schedules is supported by both Ayurvedic tradition and modern sleep hygiene research
>
> **🚫 What to Avoid:**
> - Avoid self-diagnosing or relying on online symptom checkers for medical decisions
> - Avoid strenuous exercise until you feel better
>
> **👀 What to Monitor:**
> - Track whether the headache is improving, stable, or worsening
> - Note any new symptoms such as vision changes, neck stiffness, or fever
>
> **🏥 Seek Professional Care If:**
> - Headache becomes severe or is the "worst headache of your life"
> - You develop fever, stiff neck, vision changes, or confusion
> - Headache persists beyond 48 hours or worsens significantly
> - You experience any new or concerning symptoms
>
> **Evidence Confidence:** Limited
>
> ⚕️ *MedicoBuddy provides general wellness information for educational purposes only. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatments, or replace professional medical advice.*
